A job, in order

From survey to handover

01 · Survey

We walk the site, mark dead zones, and note where cable can actually run.

02 · Design

Access-point count, switch ports, and which carrier handoff the rack should expect.

03 · Install

Cabling, mounting, power, and names on every port. No mystery leads in the ceiling.

04 · Prove it

We test the rooms you named, write down the passwords, and show one person how to restart it.

Also on the desk

After the first day

Managed changes

A new floor, a new till, or a camera that needs its own lane. We amend the design instead of stacking routers.

Failover

Where the work cannot stop, we can add a second carrier handoff. The second line is still a licensed carrier’s line.

Quiet security

Separate guest access, updated admin passwords, and no shared “password123” on the sticker under the router.

TeknTandao does not claim a Communications Authority network licence on this page. We design and install the network inside your building. Upstream internet is ordered from a licensed carrier, in your name or ours as the job requires, and that relationship is written into the quote.
