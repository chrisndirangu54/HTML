Engineering concept · Hybrid ISP

Fiber, wireless and intelligent operations

A network you can monitor, not just install.

Our proposed hybrid deployment connects a MikroTik subscriber edge to GPON optical distribution and managed COMFAST wireless backhaul. A separate monitoring and operations layer supports incident records, technician workflows and future AI-assisted fault analysis.

The 50-subscriber topology is an illustrative engineering scenario, not a claim that this infrastructure is already deployed. Device compatibility, licensed service arrangements and radio approvals need validation.
