Step 02 · Estimate

Interactive engineering calculations

These calculators provide dimensionally explicit, deterministic equations. Validate assumptions with higher-fidelity simulators, component datasheets and physical measurements.

DC series resistor calculation

Two ideal series resistors on a DC source. No temperature, tolerance, source resistance or wiring effects.

Battery runtime & daily solar energy

Idealized average-load approximation. Battery chemistry, BMS, temperature, weather and charge profiles are excluded.

Rectangular cantilever beam, end point load

Euler–Bernoulli small-deflection linear elasticity, ideal fixed support. No stress concentration, material yield limit or fatigue validation.

Two-wheel differential-drive concept

Planar no-slip kinematics, ignoring acceleration, terrain, motor torque, slip and load.

Step 05 · Critique and refine

Optional on-device AI engineering copilot

Run an open-source local inference engine when your browser supports WebGPU. The model can help brainstorm risks and test cases, while all numerical results remain deterministic and independently checkable.

Load AI locally — explicit opt-in

For capable devices, WebLLM can download a small language model and run it inside your browser. The initial model download may be large and can use significant GPU memory; no automatic download occurs.

Model not loaded. Engineering checklist features work without it.

The generated text may be inaccurate. Never use it as certification, structural sign-off, wiring authorization, fire-safety design or autonomous equipment approval.

AI response

When the local model is ready, you can request a critique. A deterministic design checklist is already available above, even without WebGPU.

Model inference uses open-source WebLLM when supported, loaded from a third-party package CDN. Queries remain on-device after assets load. AI cannot control machinery, deploy firmware or place orders.
