The rules on the ground

What changed, and who supervises it

For the latest interpretations, read the CMA's published Act and 2026 Regulations and CBK notices. This briefing is not a live licensing register.

The Act

The Virtual Asset Service Providers Act, No. 20 of 2025, commenced on 4 November 2025. It is the statute for people offering virtual-asset services in or from Kenya.

The Regulations

Legal Notice 134 of 2026, the Virtual Asset Service Providers Regulations, was gazetted on 22 July 2026. It is the licensing detail under that Act.

Two regulators

The Central Bank of Kenya supervises wallets, payment processors, and stablecoin issuers. The Capital Markets Authority supervises exchanges, brokers, advisers, managers, initial coin offerings, tokenisation, and token-issuance platforms.

NFTs that are not used for payment, investment, or another financial purpose sit outside the Act’s virtual-asset definition. A collectible can still be marketed like an investment. Read the actual use, not the label.

NFTs

Collectibles, access, and the financial line

Art and access

Kenyan artists and studios use NFTs as editions, tickets, and membership passes. That is a publishing and community tool. It is not, by itself, a market.

The financial test

If a token is sold as a way to earn, to share revenue, or to stand in for an asset people trade, treat it as something the capital-markets side of the VASP regime may cover.

Custody

A marketplace can disappear. A wallet phrase that leaks is gone. Keep the phrase offline, and never type it into a chat, including this site’s assistant.

Trading guide

How to study a trade before you place one

This is hygiene, not a signal. TeknTandao does not manage money and does not tell you what to buy.

1

Name the asset

Write down the chain, the contract or mint, and what the token does when nobody is promoting it.

2

Separate the money

Only use cash you can lose. Rent, payroll, and M-Pesa float do not belong in a trade.

3

Check the venue

As licensing opens, prefer a provider that can show the CBK or CMA permission its activity requires. A Telegram admin is not a venue.

4

Write the exit first

Decide the reason you would sell, or the reason you would walk away, before the price moves.

5

Keep your own record

Save dates, amounts in KES, and fees. Do not assume a foreign app files anything for you in Kenya.

6

Refuse the rush

Guaranteed returns, “limited slots”, and anyone asking for a seed phrase are the same scam in a new coat.

Hottest topics

What builders in Nairobi are actually arguing about

The licensing window

Operators already in market have a hard date. New products need to know which of the ten regulated activities they resemble before they take a customer’s money.

Stablecoin reserves

The Regulations put reserve, custody, and redemption rules on stablecoin issuers. A peg is a claim. The reserve is the part you can check.

Real-world assets

Tokenising land, invoices, or funds is its own licensed activity under the CMA side. A PDF and a token are not a title deed.

M-Pesa next door

Everyday payments in Kenya still run through M-Pesa. A chain product that cannot explain the on-ramp and the off-ramp is a demo.

Scam patterns

Fake support desks, copied exchange sites, and “airdrops” that ask you to connect a wallet and approve unlimited spend.

Chains

What the networks are being used for

These are public roles, not TeknTandao partnerships and not a ranking.

Bitcoin and Lightning

Bearer savings and small, fast payments. Lightning is the layer people use when the base chain is too slow or too costly for a till.

Ethereum

The default home for general smart contracts and for the NFT standards most marketplaces still speak.

Base, Arbitrum, Polygon

Ethereum environments with lower fees. Same idea of a contract and a wallet, less cost per click.

Solana

High-throughput consumer apps and mints, chosen when an experience has to feel instant.

Celo

A mobile-first network that African builders have used for stablecoin transfers and lightweight apps.

Stellar

A payments network aimed at corridors: moving value between currencies with a short path.
