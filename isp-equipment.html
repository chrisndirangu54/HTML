Fiber distinction: An Ethernet WAN router does not replace an optical GPON ONT. Low-cost Huawei or VSOL ONTs require lab-based OLT interoperability checks. TR-069 or TR-369 support does not guarantee every Wi-Fi diagnostic is exposed.

50-customer planning budget

Original concept: 32 GPON fiber homes and 18 wireless-fed customers, one PoP and two remote buildings. Physical capital costs were estimated at KSh 223,500 core, KSh 330,480 fiber, KSh 341,400 wireless and KSh 134,307 contingency, or approximately KSh 1,029,687. Reserve nearer KSh 1.15 million until VAT and site-specific costs are quoted. Major wayleaves, trenching, licensing, staff, and separate software development were not fully priced.
