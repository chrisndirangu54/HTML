Media Cloud is TeknTandao's developing AI-assisted digital marketing service, intended to bring content planning, campaign analysis, influencer research and brand sentiment into a coordinated workspace.

Discuss a media campaign Explore planned capabilities

One connected workflow

Plan, review and improve campaigns using measurable signals. Automation and publication will require platform permissions and explicit customer approval.

Development status: Media Cloud is not yet a deployed SaaS dashboard. The public repository currently contains a project overview; scheduling, integrations, AI models and analytics shown here describe the product direction rather than live functionality.

A more informed approach to digital marketing.

Media Cloud is designed to connect creative work with useful performance evidence, while keeping final publishing decisions with authorized users.

Content planning

Planned multi-channel campaign calendars, approval queues and assisted scheduling for connected accounts.

Campaign intelligence

Planned performance summaries, engagement patterns and data-informed content recommendations.

Influencer discovery

Planned suitability comparisons based on audience relevance, public signals and disclosed campaign objectives.

Sentiment insights

Planned brand and creator sentiment analysis, with human review to reduce misclassification and bias.

AI-assisted content

Planned content ideation and iterative improvement suggestions under editorial control.

BeatSync integration

A planned, permission-based link to the independently developed BeatSync Studio video workflow, without coupling the two products.

Built for responsible marketing operations.

01 · Connect approved accounts

External social platforms require official API access and customer-granted permissions; no access is assumed.

02 · Review campaign evidence

Proposed reporting will distinguish measured results from predictions and subjective sentiment classifications.

03 · Approve and publish

Publishing, audience access and marketing actions should remain subject to user authorization and platform policies.
