Open-source design ecosystem

Powerful engineering tools from GitHub

Use these established open projects to build, inspect, simulate and prepare designs. The built-in Three.js preview and OpenSCAD geometry workflow are integrated; other applications open separately and do not secretly transmit your files.

OpenCascade / replicad

Parametric BREP geometry, fillets and STEP-capable engineering solid modelling.

GitHub: replicad

ToubkalCAD

Browser-based OCCT/Three.js CAD with sketching, parametric operations and STEP exchange.

GitHub: ToubkalCAD

FreeCAD

Feature-based CAD, drawings, assemblies and manufacturing preparation.

GitHub: FreeCAD

OpenSCAD

Code-based parametric CAD. The existing plate designer exports editable OpenSCAD source.

GitHub: OpenSCAD

MeshRight

External desktop/locally hosted mesh review and repair. GPLv3; not embedded in this service.

GitHub: MeshRight

KiCad

Electronic design automation, PCB schematic/layout, ERC and manufacturing outputs.

GitHub: KiCad

Webots

Robot modelling, sensor simulation and controller testing before physical trials.

GitHub: Webots

OpenFOAM

Advanced CFD for supported high-fidelity engineering workflows; GPL licence, separate environment.

GitHub: OpenFOAM
