# Homepage media

- `assets/models/gaming-setup.glb` is the hero 3D scene. It rotates from live `scrollY` and pointer movement, and travels left-to-right while the hero is in view.
- A single floating orb (`assets/lottie/ai-robo.json`) is mounted on the page, not inside the hero widget. It moves around the viewport as you scroll and eases toward the pointer.
- `assets/models/sci-fi-ob-robot.glb` is the animated robot above the homepage footer.
- Colours and gradients are shared across the main page and inner pages. They auto-cycle unless a swatch is chosen in Configuration. The rainbow swatch turns auto-cycle back on.
