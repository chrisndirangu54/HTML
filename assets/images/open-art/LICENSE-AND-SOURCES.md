## Original CC0 illustration collection

The other SVG files under `assets/images/open-art/` (except the Nouns sample) are original vector illustrations authored for this project, including the three colourful *NFT-style concepts* `nft-robot.svg`, `nft-planet.svg`, and `nft-dream.svg`. They are **not minted NFTs**, and carry no token or ownership claims. To the extent copyright subsists in these original illustrations, they are released into the public domain under **CC0 1.0** (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

Illustrations are self-contained, include no remote image imports, and may be reused/remixed. No third-party branded logos or misleading licensing claims should be added to the artwork without appropriate permission.

## Accessibility and performance

- SVGs are local, non-animated source images with descriptive alt text.
- NFT-style animated frames and visual effects are CSS only; `prefers-reduced-motion` stops all decorative motion.
- No web wallets, blockchain services, NFT mints, client-side purchases, or rights/ownership verification are involved.
- The illustrations on Events/Gallery/Podcast/Awards pages are **decorative concepts**, not images of actual programs, episodes, awards or people.

The Web3 NFT art panel points to Nouns and this licence record, explicitly describing the CC0 art sample. An `open-visuals` attribution link appears on visually enriched public pages.
